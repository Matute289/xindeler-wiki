# Dungeons

Xindeler has two distinct kinds of dungeon.

## Story Dungeons

Some dungeons are tied to specific quests and missions — narrative-driven spaces that exist as part of the world's ongoing story, not something you stumble into by exploring. What's inside, and what it means, is part of what the game reveals as you play through it.

## World Dungeons

Other dungeons are out there to be found while exploring the world — no quest required, just the willingness to go looking. Each one carries a difficulty tier: going in under-leveled doesn't lock you out, but it will get you killed fast. Going in appropriately geared, on the other hand, is how you find the best loot and the toughest fights in the game.

> *Which specific dungeons exist, where they are, and what waits inside — that's part of the content that expands here as it's confirmed.*
