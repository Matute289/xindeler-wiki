# Dungeons

Xindeler has two distinct kinds of dungeon.

## Story Dungeons

Some dungeons are tied to specific quests and missions — narrative-driven spaces that exist as part of the world's ongoing story, not something you stumble into by exploring. What's inside, and what it means, is part of what the game reveals as you play through it.

## World Dungeons

Other dungeons are out there to be found while exploring the world — no quest required, just the willingness to go looking. Each one carries a difficulty tier: going in under-leveled doesn't lock you out, but it will get you killed fast. Going in appropriately geared, on the other hand, is how you find the best loot and the toughest fights in the game.

The ten known world dungeons:

| Dungeon | Inhabitants | Boss(es) |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| **The Wrackwood Bastion** | The Wrackroot — twisted wood-spirits bound to a fortified grove | Chieftain, Harvester |
| **The Rimehowl Hold** | The Rimehowl — a frost-bound pack that never leaves its cliffs | Elder, a bound Yeti |
| **The Drowned Shoal** | The Drowned — amphibious raiders of a sunken atoll | The Shellmaw |
| **The Claybound Ossuary** | The Claybound — ancient clay sentinels that never stopped guarding | Gravewarden |
| **Castle Sanguine** | A vampiric bloodline holed up behind its own walls | Bloodmoon Bat |
| **The Chitinfall Hive** | A myrmidon warren, disciplined and merciless | Cyclops, Minotaur |
| **The Sunken Cathedral of Dagon** | A drowned cult still keeping its old rites | Dagon |
| **The Cultist Sanctum** | A cult bound to something that eats minds | The Mind-Eater |
| **The Sundered Necropolis** | Terracotta guardians animated by a lingering curse | Cursekeeper |
| **The Cinderdeep Mine** | A dwarven forge-hold, worked long past when it should've stopped | Forgemaster |

> *Where each of these sits in the world, and the finer details of what's inside, expand here as they're confirmed in-game.*
