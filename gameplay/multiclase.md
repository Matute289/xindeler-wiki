# Multiclassing

Once a character is experienced enough, Xindeler lets you branch into a second class without giving up the first — a D&D-style multiclass system.

## How It Works

- **Level requirement:** your character needs to reach **level 20** in their primary class before a second class becomes available.
- **Maximum two classes.** You cannot pick up a third.
- **You keep progress in both.** Levels earned afterward are split between your two classes rather than lost — nothing from your primary class is reset when you take on the second.
- **Full access to both kits.** A multiclassed character can draw on the ability pool and equipment options of both classes, not just the primary one.

## Choosing a Second Class

Any playable class can be picked as a second class, as long as it's different from your first. There's no forced pairing — a Warrior can pick up Cleric for some self-sufficiency, a Mage can pick up Rogue for mobility, or you can lean into a stranger combination entirely.

## Why Multiclass

Multiclassing is for players who've already found their footing with one class and want to shape a more personal build instead of following a single class straight to the level cap. It's optional — sticking with one class the whole way is just as viable.

## Tips

- Multiclassing is a **level 20+ decision**, not a character-creation one — pick your primary class first, and treat the second as a build refinement once you know how your character plays.
- Because both classes keep advancing afterward, there's no rush to multiclass the moment you hit level 20 — it's available whenever the build calls for it.
