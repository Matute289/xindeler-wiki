# Weapons

Xindeler currently has **9 weapon types**, each with its own combat feel and skill-tree interactions. Dagger, shield, spear and blowgun are planned for a future update.

## Weapon Types

| Type | Style |
|------|-------|
| Sword | Balanced, combo-driven melee |
| Axe | Heavy, high-damage melee with cleave potential |
| Hammer | Slow, poise-breaking melee |
| Bow | Ranged physical damage |
| Staff | Ranged caster weapon (Arcana) |
| Sceptre | Support/caster weapon (Divine) |
| Tome | Caster weapon, basic spell set |
| Holy Symbol | Caster weapon, basic spell set (Divine) |
| Focus | Caster weapon, basic spell set (Arcana) |

Caster weapons (Tome, Holy Symbol, Focus, Staff, Sceptre) determine which set of basic spells you have available — see [Magic](/gameplay/magia) for how sources, schools and spells fit together.

## Modular Crafting

Weapons are **modular**: the final stats come from the combination of materials used, not a fixed template. A weapon crafted from common iron behaves very differently from the same weapon type forged with a rarer material — see [Crafting](/gameplay/crafteo) for the quality-tier system that governs this.

## Legendary Weapons

Some weapons in Xindeler are unique, storied relics rather than crafted gear. Their true history is part of the game's deeper lore — what follows is only what's safe to say out loud.

- **Frostbane** <span class="badge-secret">Lore</span> — a relic said to date back to the Great War. Whatever it cuts stays cold long after the wound closes.
- **The Leviathan's Axe** <span class="badge-secret">Lore</span> — an axe of draconic make, too large and too old for mortal hands to have forged it.
- **The Veilbinder Staff** <span class="badge-secret">Lore</span> — a staff that hums with the Veil itself. Those who've studied it don't like to talk about who last carried it.
- **Wailmaul** <span class="badge-secret">Lore</span> — a war-hammer that carries the sound of screaming faces into every swing. No one has traced where the screaming comes from.
- **The Reaver of the Crownless King** <span class="badge-secret">Lore</span> — a blade named for a throne that no longer exists, wielded by someone who very much does.
- **The Marrow-Scepter** <span class="badge-secret">Lore</span> — a scepter crowned with something that used to be a skull. It does not like being pointed at the living.

> *Where these relics are, who carries them now, and what they truly are — that's part of the story the game reveals as it unfolds.*
