# Backgrounds

Your background captures who your character was before the adventure began — their trade, their station, and the world that shaped them. Xindeler has **24 backgrounds** across 7 categories.

A background never restricts your race or class choice. A Noble can be a Barbarian, a Criminal can be a Cleric — it's purely a story hook and a starting point, not a build constraint.

## Spiritual

- **Acolyte** — trained since youth in a temple of the Luminaries (or, rarely, one of the Unfaithful).
- **Hermit** — a recluse who withdrew into solitude and found a hidden truth out there.
- **Inquisitor** — served a Luminary's tribunal hunting heretics and cultists.

## Academic

- **Sage** — a scholar of the great arcane academies or a lord's private library.
- **Archaeologist** — a delver into ruins and places lost to time.
- **Scribe** — a copyist for an academy or a noble chancery, sharp-eyed for forgeries.
- **Investigator** — gets access to people and places through fast talk and official-looking papers.

## Martial

- **Soldier** — a veteran of a city levy, a standing army, or a mercenary company.
- **Guard** — stood watch at a gate, wall or noble estate.

## Criminal

- **Criminal** — part of an underworld network, keeps a reliable contact on hand.
- **Charlatan** — a con artist with a false identity and forged papers.
- **Bounty Hunter** — tracks quarry through the city's back-channels.

## Social

- **Noble** — born into one of Xindeler's great houses, welcome wherever fine people gather.
- **Entertainer** — a traveling minstrel, acrobat or tumbler.
- **Folk Hero** — a commoner who stood up to a monster or a corrupt lord and was remembered for it.
- **Merchant** — a trader of the road and caravan circuit.

## Trade & Craft

- **Artisan** — a maker of fine goods outside any formal guild structure.
- **Farmer** — worked the land through drought, blight and harvest.
- **Fisher** — lives by the water and the catch.
- **Miner** — dug ore, stone and gemstone from the deep places of the earth.

## Wilderness

- **Outlander** — raised beyond the borders of settled law.
- **Guide** — led travelers through wild edges and trackless reaches.
- **Sailor** — a deckhand of the coastal and river trade.
- **Urchin** — a street-child who learned the city's hidden passages early.

## Choosing a Background

Backgrounds are purely a roleplay and flavor choice today — pick whichever story hook appeals to you. There's no mechanical penalty for mixing an unusual background with a given race or class.
