# Armor

Armor in Xindeler is crafted per slot — see [Crafting](/gameplay/crafteo) for how quality tiers and materials work.

## Armor Slots

| Slot | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Head | |
| Neck | |
| Shoulders | |
| Chest | |
| Hands | |
| Ring (×2) | Two independent ring slots — you can wear two different rings at once |
| Back | Cloaks, capes |
| Belt | |
| Legs | |
| Feet | |
| Tabard | A separate cosmetic/heraldic layer, independent of your chest armor |

Beyond the 12 equipment slots above, your inventory also has 4 dedicated bag slots for carrying additional storage bags.

## Armor Sets

There are over 50 armor sets in the game. The numbers below are each set's base multiplier — confirmed directly from the game's own item data — not the final stat on a specific piece, which also depends on that piece's quality tier (see [Crafting](/gameplay/crafteo)):

| Set | Identity | Protection | Poise Res. | Energy Max | Energy Reward | Precision Power | Stealth |
|-----|----------|-----------:|-----------:|-----------:|---------------:|-----------------:|--------:|
| Adventurer | Generic starting-tier gear, no class restriction | 18.2 | 10.9 | — | — | — | 0.63 |
| Assassin | Light, stealth-oriented | 50.0 | 5.0 | — | — | — | — |
| Boreal | Cold-region, heavy insulation theme | 90.0 | 36.0 | -45.0 | — | 0.45 | 0.45 |
| Cultist | Dark, ritualistic aesthetic | 72.0 | 18.0 | 27.0 | 0.36 | 0.225 | 0.36 |
| Golemite | Heavy, construct-inspired plating | 117.0 | 40.5 | 63.0 | -0.45 | — | — |
| Leather Plate | Hybrid of leather and metal plating | 120.0 | 30.0 | — | — | — | — |
| Plate | Full metal plating, precision-oriented | 51.6 | — | — | — | 0.70 | 1.31 |
| Dragonscale | Scaled armor with draconic styling | 54.0 | — | — | — | 0.72 | 1.35 |

Each set has its own class and level requirements — check the crafting recipe or the item tooltip in-game for exact numbers, which vary by piece and quality tier.

**Mail armor is a special case**: instead of a fixed named set, it scales through the same 6-tier metal ladder as weapons (Bronze → Iron → Steel → Cobalt → Silver/Gold/Bloodsteel → Orichalcum), so its stats depend entirely on which metal it's crafted from rather than a set identity.
